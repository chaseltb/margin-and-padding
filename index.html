1. Margin vs Padding

Margin (orange)
Padding (blue)
Content
Margin creates space around an element.

Padding creates space inside an element between the border and its content.
dbc.Card( dbc.CardBody("Content"), className="m-4 p-3" ) m-4 = margin p-3 = padding

2. Container

Container

Containers center your page and keep content from stretching across the whole screen.

dbc.Container([...])

3. Row

Row

A row is a horizontal strip that holds columns.

dbc.Row([ ... ])

7. Bootstrap Spacing Cheat Sheet

m = margin p = padding mt = margin-top mb = margin-bottom ms = margin-left me = margin-right mx = left & right my = top & bottom pt = padding-top pb = padding-bottom px = left & right py = top & bottom Examples m-3 mt-4 mb-5 mx-auto p-2 px-4 py-5
